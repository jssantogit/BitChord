## Evidence limits

- The XML dimensions from am-descomp are **static evidence**, not measurements of the rendered screen. In particular, the source itself marks the mapping of the 80dp action-sheet header as *unproven* for the observed sheet.
- Font-family assets, layout-specific effective font weight, line-height and exact animated easing cannot be asserted 1:1 from those tokens. Preserve existing Android fonts; do not distribute Apple font files.
- A user-uploaded screenshot and a GitHub Actions success establish appearance at that instant and compile/test validity respectively; neither proves end-to-end fidelity on a different device.
- Never label audio Lossless, Dolby Atmos or Hi-Res on user preference or catalog marketing; only renderer measurements may establish those claims. An unavailable measurement must be visually distinct from proof of lossless audio.

## Badge icon verification (8 November 2026)

- `am-descomp/ui-assets/catalog/android-vector/badges/base/drawable/ic_nowplaying_lossless.xml`: exactly **14 × 9 dp**. Compared its complete pathData (3,472 characters) with `AppleQualityVectors.Lossless`; it is already identical. Do **not** load the SVG variant on Android (caused a prior platform exception).
- `am-descomp/ui-assets/catalog/android-vector/badges/base/drawable/ic_nowplaying_dolby_atmos.xml`: exactly **83 × 15 dp**. Replaced the previous BitChord 13 × 9 dp double-D + text approximation with the full source pathData in `sharedUi/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/ic_apple_nowplaying_dolby_atmos.xml`, rendered as one 83 × 15 dp vector in `AppleDolbyAtmosBadge`.
- `Hi-Res Lossless` uses the same verified Lossless wave glyph. High/medium compressed-quality indicators retain BitChord headphones and truthful quality text; Apple did not supply verified separate glyphs for these compressed categories in the extracted badge directory.
- User preference: preserve **artwork-based blur and gradient** in the menu. `SongActionsSheet` again uses `rememberArtworkPalette(song.thumbnailUrl)` and `ArtworkBackdrop`; restored the default scrim strength after rejecting the near-black flat-sheet experiment.
- Actual 1:1 badge appearance still requires screenshots, density/font-scale normalization, and testing a real decoded Atmos or Lossless stream.
